Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Liu Qi collects and uses information submitted for private written readings.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, birth date, birth time, birth place, relationship status, one relationship question, optional context, payment selection, and add-on selections. If you purchase a compatibility add-on, we may also collect the other person’s name or initials, gender, birth date, birth time, and birth place.

How we use your information

  • To prepare and deliver your written reading.
  • To match your payment, add-ons, and submitted form details.
  • To respond to support requests or clarification requests.
  • To improve the checkout and delivery process.

Payment information

Card and PayPal payments are processed by third-party payment providers. Do not send full card details by email or through the reading form.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed for payment, form submission, email delivery, website hosting, or customer support.

Data retention

Reading details may be retained as needed to deliver your service, provide clarification, handle support requests, and maintain records.

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of your submitted information by contacting support, subject to reasonable recordkeeping and service-delivery needs.
