Can I ask unrelated questions?

No. The service covers one focus area. A new unrelated topic requires a new reading.

Do you serve non-Chinese clients?

Yes. The reading can be delivered in English and is suitable for clients outside China.

Can I buy a reading for someone else?

Yes, as long as you have permission to provide their birth details and question.

What if I do not know my exact birth time?

You can write “unknown” or provide an approximate time. The reading will be adjusted based on the information available.

Can I get a refund?

If manual work has not started, you may request cancellation. Once personalized work has started or a reply has been delivered, refunds are usually not available.

Is this professional advice?

No. Readings are for personal insight and reflection only. They do not replace medical, legal, financial, investment, or mental health advice.
