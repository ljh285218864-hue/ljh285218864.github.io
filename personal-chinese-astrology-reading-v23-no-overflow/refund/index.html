Digital custom service

Readings are customized written services. Once the reading has started or has been delivered, the order is generally not refundable.

If your reading has not started, you may request cancellation within 2 hours after payment. If you contact us before your reading has started, we may cancel and refund the order when possible.

After you submit your reading details and the reading preparation begins, refunds are generally not available because the work is personalized.

If you believe you were charged twice or paid the wrong amount, contact us with your payment receipt and delivery email.

Late delivery

If your reading is significantly delayed beyond the stated delivery window, contact us so we can review the order status.
